1. Personal-use purpose

Backyard Weather is an independent, non-commercial Windows weather-analysis project maintained by Adam Miculka. It displays personal weather-station observations, original forecasts, comparison calculations, and optional AI interpretations. This website supplies project information and policies; it is not a hosted weather dashboard, an account service, or a public offer of a managed subscription.

These terms apply to use of the supplied software and this website. Use only with devices, accounts, and records you own or are authorized to access. Do not use the software to obtain unauthorized access or interfere with another person’s devices or data.

2. Nest connection & permissions

The present Nest component is a separate authorization and read-only device-status tester. It does not implement an ongoing event collector or thermostat control. A proposed heating-cycle overlay is a development goal, not a currently available service.

Authorizing the integration permits only the disclosed processing; it is not consent to sell data, use it for advertising, or train AI models. Google access may be revoked at any time. Google’s own Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Device Access terms continue to apply. Nothing in these terms overrides provider requirements.

3. Weather information & limitations

Measurements can have missing periods, sensor limitations, timing differences, or local influences. Forecasts and experimental adjustments can be wrong. AI explanations can contain mistakes and must be evaluated against the supporting records. A successful software check is not a certification of measurement accuracy or future forecasting skill.

The app is not an emergency-warning system, an HVAC safety system, or a substitute for official severe-weather alerts. Use official guidance and appropriate professional assistance for safety-critical decisions. The current Nest tester sends no commands to change heating or cooling.
