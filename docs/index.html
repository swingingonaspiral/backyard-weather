Backyard Weather is a personal Windows application that brings weather-station records and original National Weather Service forecasts together for local comparison and analysis.

How information is handledAbout the Nest connection
Personal-use project. The weather application is available for the owner’s use. Nest support currently consists of a separate authorization and device-status tester; an ongoing heating-event collector is not yet installed. This public site provides information and policies, not access to the application or its records.

The application keeps original forecast issue times, observation times, and data-quality context visible.

01 / OBSERVE

Explore recorded conditions

Review temperature, humidity, wind, rainfall and soil-moisture readings. Browse dates, inspect individual values and distinguish missing records from recovered observations.

02 / COMPARE

Review original forecasts

Compare forecasts with observations over matching times and periods. Evaluate temperature differences and experimental, source-specific rain-probability adjustments.

03 / INVESTIGATE

Ask focused questions

Optional OpenAI analysis can use selected weather records and bounded local calculations. Optional OneDrive sharing exchanges weather records between the owner’s computers.

Optional Google Nest connection

See heating status
in its weather context.

The purpose of Nest access is to inspect the owner’s thermostat status and, in a future integration, compare recent heating cycles with nearby outdoor-temperature changes. This can help investigate whether furnace exhaust affects a weather-station reading.

Authorization happens on Google’s pages. The current tester lists authorized devices and displays thermostat temperature and heating/cooling status. It does not change thermostat settings or save a heating-history archive.

Read the specific Google-data disclosures →

What connecting means

Access is optional and limited to the home and devices you select in Google’s consent flow. The tester uses Google’s Smart Device Management permission for its read-only status calls.

The weather app’s AI and OneDrive features are separate: the Nest tester does not send Google device data to OpenAI or include it in weather-sharing packages.

Backyard Weather is not developed, endorsed, or operated by Google, Ambient Weather, NOAA/NWS, Microsoft, or OpenAI.

Private application, public information

This site has no account login, weather-upload form, advertising, or analytics scripts. It does not connect to your thermostat or expose your local application.

Hosting providers can process ordinary website requests. The privacy notice explains that distinction.

Google Limited Use commitment

Backyard Weather follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, when using or transferring Google user data.

Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models by this project.
